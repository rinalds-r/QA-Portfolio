# Product Catalog Test Cases

| ID | Title | Preconditions | Steps | Expected Result |
|----|-------|---------------|-------|-----------------|
| CAT-001 | Verify product catalog loads successfully | User is on the Home page | 1. Navigate to the Product Catalog page. | The Product Catalog page loads successfully without errors. All catalog elements are displayed correctly. | 
| CAT-002 | Verify product images are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Open the Product Catalog page.<br> 2. Review all displayed products. | Every product image is displayed correctly. No broken or missing images are present. | 
| CAT-003 | Verify product titles are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Open the Product Catalog page.<br> 2. Review all product titles. | Every product has a visible, readable, and correctly formatted title. | 
| CAT-004 | Verify product prices are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Open the Product Catalog page.<br> 2. Review product prices. | Every product displays a valid price in the correct currency and format. | 
| CAT-005 | Verify product availability is displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Open the Product Catalog page.<br> 2. Review product availability information. | Product availability is displayed correctly for each product according to its current status. | 
| CAT-006 | Verify product cards are aligned correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Open the Product Catalog page.<br> 2. Review the layout of all product cards. | Product cards are aligned consistently. No overlapping elements, broken layout, or unexpected spacing is present. | 
| CAT-007 | Verify clicking a product opens the correct Product Page | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Click any product card. | The corresponding Product Page opens successfully and displays the selected product information. | 
| CAT-008 | Verify there are no duplicated products in the catalog | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review all displayed products. | No duplicate product cards are displayed within the product catalog. | 
| CAT-009 | Verify there are no broken product images | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review all product images. | All product images load successfully without broken image icons, placeholders, or loading errors. |
