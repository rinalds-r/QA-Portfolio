# Cart Test Cases

| ID | Title | Preconditions | Steps | Expected Result |
|----|-------|---------------|-------|-----------------|
| CART-001 | Verify product catalog loads successfully | User is on the Home page | 1. Navigate to the Product Catalog page. | Product catalog loads successfully without errors. |
| CART-002 | Verify all product images are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review all product cards. | Every product image is displayed correctly. No missing or broken images are present. |
| CART-003 | Verify product titles are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review all product titles. | Every product has a visible and readable title. |
| CART-004 | Verify product descriptions are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review product descriptions. | Product descriptions are displayed correctly and are readable. |
| CART-005 | Verify product prices are displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review product prices. | Every product displays a valid price in the correct format. | 
| CART-006 | Verify product availability is displayed correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review product availability information. | Product availability is displayed correctly for each product. | 
| CART-007 | Verify product cards are aligned correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review the layout of all product cards. | Product cards are aligned consistently without overlapping or broken layout. | 
| CART-008 | Verify clicking a product opens the Product Page | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Click any product card. | The corresponding Product Page opens successfully. | 
| CART-009 | Verify product sorting works correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Select a sorting option.<br> 2. Review the product order. | Products are sorted according to the selected option. | 
| CART-010 | Verify product filters work correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Apply one or more filters.<br> 2. Review the displayed products. | Only products matching the selected filters are displayed. | 
| CART-011 | Verify pagination works correctly | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Navigate to the next page using pagination.<br> 2. Return to the previous page. | Pagination works correctly and displays the corresponding products. | 
| CART-012 | Verify there are no duplicated products | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review all displayed products. | No duplicate product cards are displayed. | 
| CART-013 | Verify there are no broken product images | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Review every product image. | All product images load successfully without placeholders or broken image icons. |
