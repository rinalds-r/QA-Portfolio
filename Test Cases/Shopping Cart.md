# Shopping Cart Test Cases

| ID | Title | Preconditions | Steps | Expected Result |
|----|-------|---------------|-------|-----------------|
| CART-001 | Verify one product can be added to the cart | User is on a Product Page | 1. Open any Product Page.<br> 2. Click the Add to Cart button.<br> 3. Open the Shopping Cart. | The selected product is added to the cart successfully. The product information is displayed correctly. |
| CART-002 | Verify multiple products can be added to the cart | User is on the Product Catalog page | 1. Add several different products to the cart.<br> 2. Open the Shopping Cart. | All selected products are displayed in the cart with correct information. |
| CART-003 | Verify product can be removed from the cart | User has at least one product in the Shopping Cart | 1. Open the Shopping Cart.<br >2. Click the Remove button for a product. | The selected product is removed from the cart successfully. The cart is updated correctly. |
| CART-004 | Verify product quantity can be updated | User has at least one product in the Shopping Cart | 1. Open the Shopping Cart.<br> 2. Change the quantity of a product.<br> 3. Observe the cart contents. | The product quantity is updated successfully according to the selected value. |
| CART-005 | Verify empty shopping cart behavior | User has at least one product in the Shopping Cart | 1. Open the Shopping Cart.<br> 2. Remove all products.  | The cart becomes empty and the appropriate empty cart message or state is displayed. |
| CART-006 | Verify cart total is calculated correctly | User has one or more products in the Shopping Cart | 1. Add products with known prices to the cart.<br> 2. Open the Shopping Cart.<br> 3. Calculate the expected total price manually.<br> 4. Compare with the displayed total. | The displayed cart total matches the calculated total price of all products.|
| CART-007 | Verify product quantity is displayed correctly in the cart | User has products in the Shopping Cart | 1. Add one or more products to the cart.<br> 2. Open the Shopping Cart. | The quantity displayed for each product matches the number of added items. |
| CART-008 | Verify Checkout button works correctly |  User has at least one product in the Shopping Cart | 1. Open the Shopping Cart.<br> 2. Click the Checkout button. | The user is redirected to the checkout page successfully. |
| CART-009 | Verify cart contents remain after page refresh | User has at least one product in the Shopping Cart | 1. Add a product to the cart.<br> 2. Open the Shopping Cart.<br> 3. Refresh the browser page. | The cart contents remain unchanged after page refresh. Added products and quantities are preserved.|
